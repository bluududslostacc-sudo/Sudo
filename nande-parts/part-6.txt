PART 6: THE FUTURE OF NANDE

No one can say with certainty what Nande will become. The black eyes are still evolving. Some days they appear almost normal, a deep black that could be mistaken for a simple genetic trait. On other days, especially after intense battles, they seem to hold entire galaxies of cursed energy within their depths.

There are theories circulating among those who study the nature of the soul and cursed energy. One theory suggests that Nande is slowly becoming a new type of special grade being — not a curse, not a pure sorcerer, but something that bridges the two. Another theory claims that the black eyes are a form of natural domain, a permanent manifestation of his inner world that has begun to leak into reality. A more extreme theory proposes that Nande is the first step toward a future where humans and curses are no longer separate categories at all.

Nande himself does not spend much time worrying about these theories. He focuses on the present. There are still people to protect. There are still curses to exorcise. There are still friends who need him to stay human enough to laugh with them, fight with them, and live alongside them.

Yet deep down, he understands that the path he walks is irreversible. The boy named Yuji Itadori is still part of him, but that boy has been reshaped by every choice, every loss, and every victory. The black eyes are the proof of that transformation. They are the mark of someone who has taken everything the jujutsu world could throw at him and refused to break.

In the end, Nande is Yuji Itadori with black-colored eyes — and so much more. He is the continuation of a story that refused to end when it was supposed to. He is the proof that even after the worst battles, something new can still be born from the ashes. And as long as those black eyes keep looking forward, the story of Nande will continue to unfold in ways that no one, not even Nande himself, can fully predict.

This is who Nande is.
This is what he has become.
And this is only the beginning.
