PART 3: THE PERSONALITY THAT REMAINS AND THE ONE THAT CHANGED

Despite the transformation of his eyes and the deepening of his power, Nande still carries the core of Yuji Itadori’s personality. He is still the same boy who values life above all else. He still believes that everyone deserves a chance to live, even those who have committed terrible acts, as long as there is a path to redemption. He still throws himself into danger without hesitation if it means saving someone else.

But the black eyes have brought with them a colder edge. Where Yuji once hesitated, Nande calculates. Where Yuji once smiled through the pain, Nande endures it in silence. The kindness is still there, but it is no longer naive. It is a kindness that has been tempered by loss after loss, battle after battle, and the constant awareness that every decision carries the weight of possible death.

Friends who have known him since before the change notice the differences most clearly in quiet moments. Yuji used to fill silence with jokes and energy. Nande can sit in silence for long periods, simply watching the world with those black eyes as if measuring how much of it is still worth protecting. When he does speak, his words carry more weight. People listen more carefully because they can feel that every sentence has been considered.

There is also a new protectiveness in him. The black eyes seem to heighten his awareness of the fragility of the people around him. He becomes almost obsessive about keeping his allies safe, sometimes to the point of pushing them away from danger even when they want to fight beside him. This has caused tension with those who care about him, because they can see that the same desire to protect that once defined Yuji has grown sharper and more absolute in Nande.

Yet for all the changes, the heart remains. Nande still gets excited about simple things — a good meal, a funny conversation, the feeling of running under an open sky. He still cares deeply about his friends. He still refuses to let the darkness of the jujutsu world completely consume him. The black eyes may make him look more like a curse than a human at times, but the soul behind them is still fighting to stay human.

This balance between the old Yuji and the new Nande is what makes him so dangerous to his enemies and so precious to his allies. He is both the boy who once said “I want to help people” and the being who will do whatever it takes to make that wish a reality.
