PART 4: THE POWER THAT FLOWS THROUGH THE BLACK EYES

The true extent of Nande’s power is still not fully understood, even by those closest to him. What is known is that the black eyes are deeply connected to the way he manipulates cursed energy. Traditional jujutsu techniques that once required complex hand signs or intense concentration now come to him with a fluidity that borders on instinct.

When Nande channels cursed energy through his eyes, the blackness intensifies. In the heat of battle, observers have reported seeing faint trails of pure black energy following the movement of his gaze, as if the eyes themselves are casting techniques. Some of his punches carry a residual effect that lingers on the target long after the physical impact has ended — a cold, draining sensation that weakens the opponent’s own cursed energy over time.

There are rumors among curse users that looking directly into Nande’s eyes for too long during a fight can cause temporary paralysis of the will. The victim does not freeze because of a binding vow or a domain expansion. They freeze because, for a brief moment, their mind is forced to confront the sheer density of experience and pain contained within those black irises. It is not a formal technique. It is simply the weight of who Nande has become.

His physical abilities have also evolved. The body that once belonged to Yuji Itadori was already exceptional — superhuman strength, speed, and durability that allowed him to fight special grade curses. In Nande, those traits have been refined. He moves with a quiet efficiency that wastes no energy. Every strike is precise. Every dodge is calculated. The black eyes give him a constant awareness of his surroundings that borders on precognition, allowing him to react to threats before they fully materialize.

Perhaps the most unsettling aspect of his power is the way it interacts with Sukuna’s residual influence. Even after the major conflicts that defined Yuji’s story, traces of the King of Curses remain. In Nande, those traces do not dominate. Instead, they seem to have been absorbed and reshaped by the black eyes. What was once a constant battle for control has become something closer to a cold alliance. Sukuna’s power is still there, but it answers to Nande’s will more readily than it ever did before.

This combination of Yuji’s original potential, the mysterious nature of the black eyes, and the tempered remnants of Sukuna’s strength makes Nande one of the most unpredictable forces in the current jujutsu landscape. Enemies who prepare for the old Yuji Itadori find themselves facing something far more dangerous.
