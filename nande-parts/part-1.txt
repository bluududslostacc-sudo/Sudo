PART 1: THE ORIGIN OF NANDE

Nande is not an ordinary person. At first glance, he appears to be a regular high school student with a kind heart and an unbreakable will, but the truth runs much deeper. Nande is, in essence, Yuji Itadori — the same vessel of Sukuna, the same boy who swallowed the cursed finger and changed the course of the jujutsu world forever. Yet something fundamental has shifted in his being.

Where Yuji Itadori’s eyes once shone with a warm, determined brown, Nande’s eyes are pure black. Not the soft darkness of night, but a deep, bottomless black that seems to absorb light itself. These eyes do not merely look at the world; they pierce through it. People who meet his gaze often feel as if their very soul is being measured, weighed, and understood in a single moment.

This change did not happen overnight. It began after a series of intense battles that pushed Yuji beyond the limits of what a human body and soul should endure. The repeated possession by Sukuna, the deaths of friends, the overwhelming pressure of being the vessel of the King of Curses — all of it left scars that could not be healed by conventional means. Somewhere along the way, Yuji’s spirit fractured and reformed into something new. That something new is Nande.

Nande still carries Yuji’s memories, his kindness, his refusal to let anyone die on his watch. But the black eyes mark the difference. They are the physical manifestation of a soul that has stared too long into the abyss of cursed energy and refused to look away. The blackness is not emptiness — it is density. It is the concentration of every emotion, every fight, every loss, compressed into a single pair of irises that no longer reflect the world the way normal eyes do.

Those who knew Yuji before the change often say that looking into Nande’s eyes feels like looking at the same person and a complete stranger at the same time. The smile is the same. The voice is the same. The way he clenches his fists when he’s angry is the same. But the eyes… the eyes belong to someone who has seen the true face of curses and decided that running was never an option.

This is only the beginning of understanding who Nande truly is.
