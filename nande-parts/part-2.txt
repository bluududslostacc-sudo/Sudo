PART 2: THE BLACK EYES AND THEIR MEANING

The black eyes of Nande are more than a cosmetic change. They are a symbol, a warning, and a power all at once. In the world of jujutsu, the eyes of a sorcerer often reveal the depth of their connection to cursed energy. Gojo Satoru’s Six Eyes allowed him to perceive the world with unmatched clarity. Geto’s eyes carried the weight of his ideals. Sukuna’s eyes burned with pure malice.

Nande’s black eyes are different. They do not grant him a special technique in the traditional sense, but they change the way cursed energy flows through him. The darkness in his gaze acts like a gravitational pull for residual cursed energy in the environment. Curses that come near him feel an instinctive fear, as if the very presence of those eyes is a reminder that their existence is temporary.

People who have fought alongside Nande report strange phenomena. When he is truly serious, the black in his eyes seems to deepen, and a faint, almost invisible aura of pure black energy leaks from the corners of his eyelids. It is not the pinkish-red of Sukuna’s power, nor the bright blue of Gojo’s techniques. It is something quieter, heavier, and far more personal.

Some theorists among the higher-ups of jujutsu society believe that the black eyes are the result of Yuji’s soul partially merging with residual fragments of other cursed spirits he has defeated. Others claim it is the physical mark left when Sukuna’s influence was finally forced into a permanent state of coexistence rather than domination. A few even whisper that Nande is no longer purely human — that the black eyes are the first visible sign of a new kind of being, something between sorcerer and curse.

Nande himself does not talk about his eyes often. When asked, he usually just shrugs and says, “They’re just eyes.” But those who know him well can see the truth. He knows exactly what they mean. Every time he looks in a mirror, he is reminded that the boy named Yuji Itadori is still there, but he is no longer the only one looking back.

The black eyes are both a burden and a strength. They make people fear him more easily, but they also make him harder to deceive. Lies feel thinner when spoken under that gaze. Hidden cursed techniques struggle to remain invisible. And when Nande decides that someone must be protected at all costs, those black eyes become the last thing an enemy sees before the fight ends.
