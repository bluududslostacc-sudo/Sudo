PART 5: RELATIONSHIPS AND THE WORLD’S REACTION

The people who once stood beside Yuji Itadori have had to adjust to the presence of Nande. Some adapted quickly. Others needed time. The change in his eyes was the first and most obvious signal that the person they knew had evolved, but the deeper shifts in personality and power took longer to understand.

Megumi Fushiguro was among the first to notice the difference beyond the physical. He has always been perceptive, and the quiet intensity that now surrounds Nande did not escape him. Their friendship remains strong, but there is a new layer of mutual respect mixed with caution. Megumi knows that the boy who once needed guidance has become someone who might one day need to be stopped if the darkness in those black eyes ever grows too deep.

Nobara Kugisaki’s reaction was more straightforward. She still teases him, still calls him out when he gets too serious, and still stands ready to fight beside him. But even she admits that looking into Nande’s eyes for too long makes her feel like she’s staring at something that has lived through more lifetimes than a single high school student should. Their bond is still loud and full of energy, but it carries an undercurrent of understanding that the stakes have risen.

The higher-ups of jujutsu society view Nande with a mixture of interest and fear. Some see him as a potential weapon more powerful than anything they have controlled before. Others see him as a threat that must eventually be contained. The black eyes make it impossible for them to pretend he is still just another student. He is a living reminder that the line between sorcerer and something else can blur in ways they cannot fully predict or control.

Curses themselves react differently to Nande than they did to Yuji. Weaker curses often flee on sight. Stronger ones approach with a mixture of curiosity and aggression, as if sensing that the black eyes represent both a challenge and a possible evolution of their own kind. The presence of Nande has begun to shift the balance of power in subtle ways across the jujutsu world.

Through all of this, Nande continues to walk the same path he chose long ago — the path of protecting those who cannot protect themselves. The black eyes have changed how the world sees him, and how he sees the world, but the core decision remains the same. He will keep fighting. He will keep standing between the people he cares about and the darkness that wants to consume them. And as long as those black eyes remain open, that fight will not end.
